# Listing text - AKForeverRecipes

**Summary (one line):** Every recipe of every crafting profession: which ones each of your characters has, where the rest come from - trainer, vendor and price, quest, drop - whether the recipe is BoP or BoE, what it takes to craft, and where to train the next rank.

## Description

**Recipes** - for WoW: Forever.

Your profession windows list the recipes you already have. This is the rest: about 2,170 recipes of nine
professions - Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring, Cooking, First Aid
and Mining - and where every one of them comes from.

- **A card per recipe**, like an achievement: icon, name, what it is or does, where the recipe comes from, and
  the skill it needs. Green when you have it, gold when your skill can learn it now, grey when not yet.
- **Tags** on every card: Trainer, Vendor, Quest, Drop - and **BoP** (the crafter has to get it) or **BoE**
  (mail it to an alt, buy it at auction).
- **Click a card** and it opens: the made item's stats a line each, the **reagents** with how many of each
  you have and how many crafts that is, then every source - each vendor with zone and price, the quest, the
  creatures that drop it with their chance.
- **Hover a card** for the made item's own tooltip.
- **Where to train.** The first card of every profession is its next rank - Journeyman, Expert, Artisan -
  and where your faction learns it: the trainers, the book and who sells it, the quest. Specializations too.
- **Every profession has a tab**, the ones you do not have included - to see what a profession would make,
  and where to learn it.
- **All your characters.** Each character's recipes are kept when you log it in; the name button walks
  through them, and an opened card says which of your characters has the recipe and which still misses it.
- **Missing / All / Known / Hidden** lists; a search over names, zones, NPCs, quests and reagents (`linen
  cloth` lists everything made of it; `bop`, `quest`, `vendor`, `drop` list by tag).
- **Hide what you do not care about** with a right-click; the Hidden list brings it back.
- Recipes only the other faction can get are left out, so "missing" is a real to-do list.

About 220 of Forever's new recipes have no known source anywhere yet. When one of your characters opens a
merchant who sells one, the merchant, the zone and the price are written down for all your characters.

It only reads: nothing of Blizzard's is hooked, moved or hidden.

## Commands

`/akr` (or `/recipes`):

- `/akr` - the window
- `/akr show all | known | missing | hidden` - the window on that list
- `/akr find <recipe, place, NPC or reagent>` - the matching recipes of every profession, in chat
- `/akr diag` - a report into the settings file

## The data

Recipes and sources: foreverchanges.pro. How recipe items bind and where the ranks are trained: Wowhead's
Forever pages. Further sources: the QuestieDB Forever database. A source found outside the profession's own
page says so on its card; sources carried over from Classic are marked "not confirmed for Forever yet".
