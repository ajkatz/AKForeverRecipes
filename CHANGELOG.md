# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - unreleased

- The book: every cooking recipe of WoW: Forever (123, 42 of them new) with the skill it needs and where it
  comes from - trainer, vendors with zone and price, quests, drops.
- Which recipes you have, asked of the client at login and whenever a trade skill window opens; a recipe
  learned while you play is heard at once.
- Every character's book is kept account-wide: the window walks through your characters and an opened
  recipe says who else has it or still misses it.
- Recipes only the other faction can get are left out.
- A merchant seen selling a recipe is written down - the way recipes without a published source get one.
- `/acb` (window), `/acb find`, `/acb diag`.
